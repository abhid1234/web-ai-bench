§5. Critical path summary

The four long poles:
  1. Pitch 1 trademark license is the long pole. Start the legal track in week 3 in parallel with charter drafting. If you start it in week 7, the launch slips 4–6 weeks.
  2. Pitch 3 cross-org alignment needs a GM/VP sponsor on day 1. Partnerships PM cannot get Vertex AI PM and Firebase PM to a table without Lead PM or the GM calling the meeting. This is the first action for Pitch 3, not month 3.
  3. AI Engineer World's Fair CFP may already be open or closed. Check ai.engineer immediately — this is time-sensitive and if the window is gone, the earliest conference moment for Pitch 2 is PyTorch Conference (October).
  4. GitHub Actions Marketplace review is not the bottleneck — partner alignment is. Actions review is largely automated. The bottleneck for Pitch 2's launch partner is getting Harness or GitHub to a co-announcement MOU — start outreach in week 1.
